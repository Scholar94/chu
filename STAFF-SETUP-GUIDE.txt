CHURCH MANAGER — STAFF SETUP GUIDE
==================================

ONLY THE OWNER CREATES STAFF ACCOUNTS.

1. Owner signs in.
2. Open Staff Accounts.
3. Enter staff name.
4. Create a unique username.
5. Create a strong password of at least 8 characters.
6. Choose the role.
7. Give the staff member their own credentials privately.

ROLES
Admin — broad operational access except Owner-only controls.
Secretary — members, attendance, events and announcements.
Treasurer — giving and reports.
Worker — members and attendance.

PASSWORD RULE
Never send passwords in a public group chat. If a password is forgotten, the Owner should reset it.

ACCOUNT RULE
Never create one shared username such as reception for several people. Individual accounts make the audit trail useful.
